# 🛒 Supermart Grocery Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Supermart Grocery Sales Analysis** project focuses on analyzing retail grocery sales data to identify sales trends, profitable product categories, and business performance insights.

The project uses **Excel** for data cleaning, analysis, PivotTables, calculations, and visualization. The analysis helps understand which categories and products contribute most to sales and profit and how discounts affect profitability.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Analyze overall **Sales and Profit** performance.
2. Identify the **product categories contributing the highest sales**.
3. Analyze **sales trends over time**.
4. Identify products with **high sales but comparatively low profitability**.
5. Analyze the **relationship between discount and profit**.
6. Generate meaningful business insights from the dataset.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains retail grocery sales transactions with information such as:

- Order ID
- Customer
- Category
- Sub Category
- City
- Order Date
- Region
- Sales
- Discount
- Profit
- State

The dataset contains approximately **50,000 retail transactions**.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Overall Sales & Profit Analysis

Calculated the overall business performance:

- **Total Sales:** ₹14,956,982
- **Total Profit:** ₹3,747,121.20

These KPIs provide an overview of the company's overall sales and profitability.

---

### 2. Category-wise Sales Analysis

Analyzed sales contribution across different product categories.

The category with the highest sales was:

**Eggs, Meat & Fish — ₹2,267,401**

This indicates that Eggs, Meat & Fish is the strongest contributor to overall sales among the analyzed categories.

---

### 3. Sales Trend Analysis

Analyzed sales over time using the Order Date.

The analysis helps identify:

- Monthly sales patterns
- Yearly sales trends
- Periods of higher and lower sales
- Changes in business performance over time

---

### 4. High Sales vs Low Profitability

The top-selling products were analyzed using **Profit Margin** to identify products that generate strong sales but comparatively lower profitability.

The products identified as having relatively weaker profitability among the top-selling products were:

| Product | Sales | Profit | Profit Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Spices | ₹672,876 | ₹160,302.60 | 23.82% |
| Masalas | ₹697,480 | ₹168,999.11 | 24.23% |
| Cakes | ₹685,612 | ₹168,398.46 | 24.56% |

These products may require further investigation into pricing, discounts, and costs to improve profitability.

---

### 5. Discount vs Profit Analysis

The relationship between discount and profit was analyzed to understand whether higher discounts have a noticeable impact on profitability.

The analysis can help the business evaluate whether discount strategies are contributing positively to sales while maintaining healthy profit margins.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Eggs, Meat & Fish** generated the highest sales among the analyzed categories.
- High sales do not always indicate high profitability.
- **Spices, Masalas, and Cakes** showed comparatively lower profit margins among the top-selling products.
- Profitability should be evaluated along with sales when making product and pricing decisions.
- Discount strategies should be monitored to ensure that increased sales do not negatively affect profit margins.
- Time-based sales analysis can help identify seasonal and monthly sales patterns.
